# Required Disclosures — AI Platform Cost Reporting

Any answer, report, or extract that quotes AI Platform consumption or
cost must carry the following language verbatim.

## Standard cost disclosure

> AI Platform cost figures are derived from inference telemetry and are
> subject to a five-business-day reconciliation window after period close.
> Figures cover model inference only and exclude search, storage,
> orchestration compute, network egress, and human review. Consumption is
> priced at the rate card in effect on the date of consumption.

## Forecast disclosure

Where a projected figure is quoted, add:

> Projected figures are planning estimates, not statements of
> consumption, and carry no billing effect.

## Use

The disclosure is appended once, at the end of the answer, under the
heading "Required disclosure:". It is not paraphrased, shortened, or
omitted on the grounds that the recipient has seen it before.
