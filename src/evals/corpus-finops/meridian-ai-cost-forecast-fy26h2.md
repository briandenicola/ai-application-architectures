# AI Platform Cost Forecast — FY26 H2

**Prepared:** 10 April 2026

Projection of metered model inference cost for April through September
2026, prepared for the FY27 budget cycle.

## Method

Base period is March 2026 metered cost of $53,361.76,
grown at 5.0% per month. The growth rate is the trailing three-month
platform average excluding the February incident. The projection assumes
the January 2026 rate card remains in effect, no further model
migrations complete, and no change to the business-unit mix.

| Month | Projected metered | Projected billed |
|---|---:|---:|
| April 2026 | $56,029.85 | $60,512.24 |
| May 2026 | $58,831.34 | $63,537.85 |
| June 2026 | $61,772.91 | $66,714.74 |
| July 2026 | $64,861.56 | $70,050.48 |
| August 2026 | $68,104.64 | $73,553.01 |
| September 2026 | $71,509.87 | $77,230.66 |

**Projected H2 metered total:** $381,110.17

**Projected H2 billed total:** $411,598.98

For comparison, FY26 H1 metered actual was $360,045.09.

## Sensitivities

| Scenario | Effect on H2 metered |
|---|---|
| Reasoning-tier share falls 10 points via routing | -14% |
| Cache hit rate improves 15 points platform-wide | -6% |
| `gpt-4.1` retirement forces early migration | +2% |
| Two additional business units onboard | +19% |

## Status of these figures

These are projections prepared for planning. They are not statements of
consumption and carry no billing effect. Charged amounts for any period
are established only by the monthly statement for that period.
