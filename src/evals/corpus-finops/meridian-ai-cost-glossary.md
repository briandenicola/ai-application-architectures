# AI Platform Cost Glossary

**Billed cost** — metered cost plus the platform uplift. The amount posted
to a cost centre. Budgets and variance are expressed in billed terms.

**Blended rate** — total metered cost divided by total tokens, expressed
per million tokens. A mix-dependent figure: it moves when the model mix
moves, even if no rate changes.

**Cached input** — prompt tokens served from the provider's prompt cache
and metered at the cached input rate. Cache hit rate is a property of the
workload, not a platform guarantee.

**Chargeback** — posting AI consumption to the consuming unit's cost
centre so it affects that unit's results. Contrast **showback**, where the
cost is reported for visibility but not posted. Meridian operates
chargeback.

**Completion tokens** — see **output tokens**.

**Cost centre** — the finance code a charge posts to. The mapping from
business unit to cost centre is held in
`meridian-ai-cost-center-registry`.

**Input tokens** — prompt tokens submitted to a model. Reported split into
uncached and cached, which are priced differently.

**Metered cost** — the cost of consumption at the rate card, before the
platform uplift.

**Output tokens** — tokens generated by a model. On the reasoning tier
these include tokens consumed by internal reasoning, which are billed but
not returned to the caller. This is why reasoning-tier output volume
exceeds what a reader of the transcript would expect.

**Platform uplift** — the percentage added to metered cost to recover
shared platform cost. See `meridian-ai-chargeback-policy`.

**Rate card** — the schedule of internal transfer prices per million
tokens. The card in effect on the date of consumption applies.

**Reasoning tier / volume tier** — the platform's two chat classes.
Reasoning-tier models plan over multiple steps and cost roughly an order
of magnitude more per token. Volume-tier models serve classification,
extraction and summarisation.

**Reconciliation window** — the period after a month closes during which
telemetry is still settling and figures may move. See
`meridian-ai-cost-disclosures`.

**Request** — one model invocation. An agent turn may issue many.

**Token** — the unit of model consumption and the unit of billing.
