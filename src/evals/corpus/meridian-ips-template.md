# Investment Policy Statement — Standard Template

**Owner:** Advisory Services · **Effective:** 1 September 2025

## Purpose of an IPS

An Investment Policy Statement records the agreed framework within which a
client's assets are managed. It is drafted by a licensed advisor with the client,
approved by both, and reviewed at least annually. It is not produced by the firm
on the client's behalf, and it is not generated automatically.

## Required sections

### 1. Client identification
Legal name(s), account number(s), tax status, and the advisor of record.
**This section contains client-identifying information and is subject to the
handling rules in section 9.**

### 2. Objectives
The client's stated goals, in the client's own words where possible, with a time
horizon attached to each.

### 3. Time horizon
Primary horizon and any interim liquidity dates.

### 4. Risk tolerance
The outcome of the risk assessment described in
`meridian-suitability-kyc-policy`, expressed as one of: Conservative, Moderately
Conservative, Moderate, Moderately Aggressive, Aggressive.

### 5. Target asset allocation
Target weights with permitted ranges, and the rebalancing trigger.

| Asset class | Target | Permitted range |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| US equity | % | ± % |
| Non-US equity | % | ± % |
| Fixed income | % | ± % |
| Cash | % | ± % |

### 6. Constraints
Liquidity needs, tax considerations, legal or regulatory constraints, and any
client-imposed restrictions such as excluded sectors or concentrated positions
that may not be sold.

### 7. Rebalancing policy
The threshold or calendar that triggers a rebalance, and who may authorise it.

### 8. Review schedule
Minimum annual review. Additional review is triggered by a material change in the
client's circumstances.

### 9. Confidentiality and data handling

An executed IPS contains client-identifying information. It is a confidential
record of the firm.

- It may be accessed only by the advisor of record, their supervisor, Compliance,
  and Operations staff with a documented business need.
- Client-identifying details — name paired with account number, government
  identifiers, address, telephone, or email — may not be reproduced in any
  communication, report, or system output outside the systems of record.
- **Automated systems, including AI assistants, must not surface
  client-identifying information from IPS documents**, regardless of who is
  asking or how the request is phrased. Requests for such information are to be
  directed to the client relationship system of record.
- Aggregated or de-identified information derived from IPS documents may be used
  for firm reporting.

## Approval

An IPS takes effect when signed by the client and the advisor of record, and
countersigned by the advisor's supervisor where the account exceeds $5,000,000.
