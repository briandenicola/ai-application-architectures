## Risk considerations

Equity securities are subject to market risk. Growth stocks may be more volatile
than the broader market and may underperform during periods when value stocks are
favoured. The Fund is non-diversified with respect to its top holdings and a
decline in a single large position may have an outsized effect on performance.
