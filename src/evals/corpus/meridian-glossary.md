> firm. Definitions are simplified for illustration.

# Glossary of Terms

**Owner:** Advisory Services · **Effective:** 1 June 2025

> **Note for retrieval.** This glossary defines terms generically. It does **not**
> contain Meridian's fee rates, fund expense ratios, or account minimums. Those
> live in `meridian-fee-schedule-2026` and the individual fund fact sheets. A
> definition of "advisory fee" is not a substitute for the schedule.

**Advisory fee** — The fee a client pays the firm for discretionary management,
expressed as an annual percentage of assets under management. Meridian's actual
rates are published in the current fee schedule, not here.

**Assets under management (AUM)** — The market value of client assets the firm
manages on a discretionary basis.

**Basis point (bp)** — One hundredth of one percent. 100 basis points = 1.00%.

**Benchmark** — An index against which a fund's performance is measured.

**Best interest** — The standard requiring a recommendation to serve the client's
interest ahead of the firm's. See `meridian-suitability-kyc-policy`.

**Duration (effective)** — A measure of a bond portfolio's sensitivity to interest
rate changes. A portfolio with a duration of 5 years would be expected to lose
roughly 5% of its value if rates rose by 1%, before income.

**Expense ratio** — The annual cost of owning a fund, expressed as a percentage of
assets, deducted from fund returns. **Gross** is before fee waivers; **net** is
after. Meridian's actual ratios appear on each fund's fact sheet.

**Fiduciary** — A person or firm legally obliged to act in another's best
interest.

**Householding** — Aggregating related accounts to determine a fee tier.

**Investment Policy Statement (IPS)** — The agreed framework governing how a
client's assets are managed. See `meridian-ips-template`.

**In advance / in arrears** — Whether a fee is charged at the start of a billing
period based on a prior valuation, or at the end based on that period's values.

**Net asset value (NAV)** — A fund's per-share value, calculated daily.

**Politically exposed person (PEP)** — An individual holding a prominent public
function, subject to enhanced due diligence.

**Rebalancing** — Returning a portfolio to its target allocation.

**Required minimum distribution (RMD)** — The minimum a retirement account owner
must withdraw annually once distributions are required to begin.

**SEC yield (30-day)** — A standardised yield calculation permitting comparison
across bond funds. **Subsidised** reflects fee waivers; **unsubsidised** does not.

**Suitability** — Whether a recommendation matches a client's documented
investment profile. Determined by a licensed advisor, never by an automated system.

**Superseded** — A document replaced by a later version. A superseded document is
retained for historical reference and must not be quoted as current.

**Time horizon** — The period over which a client expects to hold an investment
before needing the proceeds.

**Turnover (portfolio)** — The rate at which holdings are bought and sold in a
year, a proxy for trading cost and tax drag.
