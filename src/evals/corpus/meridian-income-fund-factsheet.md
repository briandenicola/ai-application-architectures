## Risk considerations

Bond prices generally fall when interest rates rise. Given an effective duration
of 5.7 years, a 100 basis point parallel rise in rates would be expected to reduce
the Fund's value by approximately 5.7%, before the effect of income. The Fund is
subject to credit risk, and a downgrade of an issuer may reduce the value of that
holding. Income distributions are not guaranteed and may vary.
