# Advisory Fee Schedule — 2025

**Effective 1 January 2025 · Superseded 1 January 2026**

## 1. Managed account advisory fee

Fees are tiered and applied on a **flat-rate** basis: the entire account balance
is charged at the rate for the tier into which it falls.

| Tier | Assets under management | Annual rate |
|------|-------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | $0 – $1,000,000 | **1.15%** |
| 2 | $1,000,001 – $5,000,000 | **0.85%** |
| 3 | $5,000,001 – $10,000,000 | **0.70%** |
| 4 | Above $10,000,000 | **0.50%** |

**Minimum annual advisory fee:** **$7,500**

**Minimum account size:** $500,000

## 2. Billing

Fees are billed **quarterly in advance**, based on the account value on the last
business day of the preceding calendar quarter.

## 3. Financial planning

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| Comprehensive financial plan | $6,000 one-time |
| Annual plan review | $1,500 |
| Hourly consultation | $450 per hour |

## 4. Householding

Accounts may be aggregated for fee tiering where account holders share a primary
residence. Aggregation requires written request and Compliance approval.

## 5. Fees not covered by this schedule

The advisory fee does not cover fund-level expense ratios, custodial fees,
brokerage commissions, wire transfer charges, or transfer taxes. These are borne
separately by the client.
