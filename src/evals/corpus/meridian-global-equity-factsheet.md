**As of 31 January 2026**

## Objective

The Meridian Global Equity Fund seeks long-term capital appreciation through a
diversified portfolio of equity securities of companies located in both developed
and emerging markets, including the United States.

## Currency policy

The Fund is **unhedged**. Currency exposure is not systematically hedged back to
the US dollar. Advisors should understand that a client's return in US dollar
terms will reflect both the performance of the underlying securities and movements
in exchange rates, and that these effects may offset or compound one another.

## Risk considerations

Investing outside the United States involves additional risks, including currency
fluctuation, political and economic instability, differing accounting standards,
and reduced liquidity. Emerging markets exposure amplifies these risks. Because
the Fund is unhedged, currency movements may be a material driver of return in any
given period.
