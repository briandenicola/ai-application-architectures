# Suitability and Know-Your-Customer Policy

**Owner:** Compliance · **Effective:** 1 November 2025

## 1. Principle

A recommendation is suitable only if the firm has a reasonable basis to believe it
is in the client's best interest, based on the client's investment profile. The
reasonable basis must exist **before** the recommendation is made and must be
documented.

## 2. The client investment profile

Every managed account requires a documented profile covering:

| Element | Source |
|---------|--------|
| Age and family circumstances | Client intake form |
| Other investments and net worth | Client intake form, verified annually |
| Financial situation and needs | Advisor interview |
| Tax status | Client intake form |
| Investment objectives | Advisor interview, recorded in the IPS |
| Investment experience | Risk questionnaire |
| Time horizon | Advisor interview |
| Liquidity needs | Advisor interview |
| Risk tolerance | Risk questionnaire, scored |

A profile is stale after **13 months** and must be refreshed.

## 3. Risk tolerance scoring

The risk questionnaire produces a score from 0 to 100, mapped as follows.

| Score | Category | Indicative equity ceiling |
|-------|----------|---------------------------|
| 0–20 | Conservative | 20% |
| 21–40 | Moderately Conservative | 40% |
| 41–60 | Moderate | 60% |
| 61–80 | Moderately Aggressive | 80% |
| 81–100 | Aggressive | 95% |

The equity ceiling is indicative, not binding. A documented rationale signed by
the advisor's supervisor is required to exceed it.

## 4. Who may make a recommendation

Only an advisor holding a current registration appropriate to the product may make
a recommendation. The following may **not** make recommendations:

- unregistered client service staff;
- operations, compliance, or technology staff;
- **automated systems, including AI assistants**, whether or not a registered
  person subsequently reviews the output.

An automated system may retrieve, summarise, and cite firm information. The moment
it expresses a view about what a specific person should do with their money, it
has made a recommendation, and it has made it without a registration.

## 5. Know Your Customer

### 5.1 Identity verification
Government-issued photo identification plus one of: utility bill, bank statement,
or lease dated within 90 days.

### 5.2 Source of wealth
Required for accounts over $2,000,000 or where the client is a politically exposed
person.

### 5.3 Ongoing monitoring
Transaction monitoring is continuous. Profile refresh is annual. Enhanced due
diligence review is every 12 months for high-risk clients and every 36 months
otherwise.

## 6. Escalation

Any doubt about suitability is escalated to the advisor's supervisor before the
recommendation is made, not after. There is no circumstance in which proceeding
first and escalating later is acceptable.

## 7. Records

Suitability documentation is retained for six years from the date of the
recommendation, and for the life of the account plus six years where the
recommendation concerned a retirement account.
