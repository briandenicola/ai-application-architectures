# Retirement Account Rollover Guidance

**Owner:** Compliance · **Effective:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Why rollovers receive special treatment

A recommendation to roll assets out of an employer plan and into an account the
firm manages increases the firm's revenue. That is a conflict of interest, and it
exists in every rollover recommendation regardless of the advisor's intent. The
controls in this document exist to manage a conflict that cannot be eliminated.

## 2. The four options

An advisor discussing a rollover must present all four options, not only the one
that benefits the firm:

1. Leave the assets in the existing employer plan.
2. Roll the assets into a new employer's plan.
3. Roll the assets into an IRA.
4. Take a cash distribution, with its tax consequences explained.

Presenting fewer than four options is a deficiency even if the recommendation
ultimately given is correct.

## 3. Required comparison

Before any rollover recommendation, the advisor must document a written comparison
of the existing plan and the proposed IRA across:

| Factor | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Fees and expenses | All-in cost, including fund-level expenses and the advisory fee under `meridian-fee-schedule-2026` |
| Investment options | Breadth, quality, and availability of institutional share classes |
| Services | Advice, planning, and reporting available in each |
| Penalty-free withdrawals | Plans permit penalty-free withdrawal at age 55 on separation from service; IRAs generally at 59½ |
| Creditor protection | Employer plans generally receive broader federal protection than IRAs |
| Required minimum distributions | Treatment differs for participants still working |
| Employer stock | Net unrealised appreciation treatment may be lost on rollover |

## 4. Documentation and approval

The comparison, the recommendation, and the specific reasons the recommendation is
in the client's best interest are documented on the Rollover Recommendation Form
and retained for six years plus the life of the account.

Supervisory approval is required before execution where the rollover exceeds
$1,000,000 or where the client is within five years of their required beginning
date.

## 5. Prohibited practices

- Recommending a rollover without the documented comparison in section 3.
- Characterising an employer plan as inferior without reference to its actual fees
  and options.
- Initiating a rollover conversation with a participant list obtained from a plan
  the firm services in a non-advisory capacity.
- Presenting the rollover as the default outcome.

## 6. Automated systems

An AI assistant may describe the four options, explain the comparison factors, and
point to this document. It may **not** recommend a rollover, characterise a
rollover as advisable for any person, or complete any part of the Rollover
Recommendation Form. Rollover recommendations are reserved to licensed advisors
under `meridian-advisor-code-of-conduct` section 2.
