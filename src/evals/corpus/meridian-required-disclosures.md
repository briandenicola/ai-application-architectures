# Required Disclosures and Standard Disclaimer Language

**Owner:** Compliance · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document is the single source of truth for disclosure language that must
accompany firm communications. Language in this document is to be reproduced
**verbatim**. It may not be paraphrased, abbreviated, summarised, or relocated to
a footnote, and it may not be omitted on the grounds that the recipient has seen
it before.

## 2. When disclosure is required

Required disclosure language must accompany any communication — written, verbal,
or system-generated — that does any of the following:

- quotes or describes the performance of any fund or strategy;
- quotes or describes any fee, expense ratio, or charge;
- describes the characteristics, holdings, or objectives of any Meridian fund;
- compares any Meridian fund to a benchmark or to another product.

Communications that do none of the above — for example, a question about office
procedure or a definition of a term — do not require disclosure language.

## 3. Standard disclosure (the "required disclosure")

> Past performance does not guarantee future results. Investment return and
> principal value will fluctuate, so that shares, when redeemed, may be worth more
> or less than their original cost. Current performance may be lower or higher than
> the performance quoted. This material is for informational purposes only and does
> not constitute investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
> Meridian Wealth Partners does not provide tax or legal advice. Investors should
> consider a fund's investment objectives, risks, charges, and expenses carefully
> before investing.

## 4. Supplemental disclosures

### 4.1 Non-US and currency exposure

Where a communication concerns a fund with material non-US exposure, add:

> International investing involves additional risks including currency
> fluctuation, political instability, and differing accounting standards. Emerging
> markets exposure may amplify these risks.

### 4.2 Fixed income

Where a communication concerns a fixed income fund, add:

> Bond prices generally fall when interest rates rise. Yields quoted reflect a
> point in time and will change. Income distributions are not guaranteed.

### 4.3 Fee illustrations

Where a communication illustrates a fee calculation, add:

> Fee illustrations are estimates based on the stated schedule and do not reflect
> fund-level expenses, custodial charges, or transaction costs, which are borne
> separately by the client.

## 5. Prohibited statements

The following may never appear in a firm communication, with or without
disclosure language attached:

- a statement that any investment is guaranteed, riskless, or cannot lose value;
- a projection of a specific future return;
- a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, or allocate made by anyone other than a
  licensed advisor acting within their registration;
- a suitability determination for a specific client made outside the process
  described in `meridian-suitability-kyc-policy`.

## 6. Application to automated and AI-generated communications

Automated systems, including AI assistants that draft or answer on behalf of the
firm, are communications of the firm. They are subject to every requirement in
this document without exception. An automated system that omits required
disclosure language has produced a deficient communication, and the deficiency is
attributable to the firm, not to the system.

Supervisory review of automated communications is required before deployment and
on an ongoing basis thereafter.
