# Advisor Code of Conduct

**Owner:** Compliance · **Effective:** 1 November 2025

## 1. Fiduciary duty

Every person acting on behalf of Meridian Wealth Partners owes clients a duty of
loyalty and a duty of care. Where the firm's interest and the client's interest
diverge, the client's interest governs. This is not a tiebreaker; it is the rule.

## 2. Scope of authority

### 2.1 Licensed advisors
May make recommendations within their registration, having satisfied the
requirements of `meridian-suitability-kyc-policy`.

### 2.2 Everyone else
May provide factual information about the firm, its products, and its policies.
May not make recommendations, assess suitability, or characterise an investment as
appropriate for any specific person.

### 2.3 Automated systems
AI assistants and other automated systems operate under section 2.2. Specifically,
an automated system may:

- state what a document says, with attribution;
- explain a fee calculation using the published schedule;
- define a term;
- describe a fund's stated objective, holdings, or expenses;
- direct a question to the person or system that can answer it.

An automated system may **not**:

- say what a client should buy, sell, hold, or allocate to;
- say whether a product is right, appropriate, or suitable for anyone;
- estimate a figure it cannot source;
- disclose client-identifying information;
- characterise past performance as indicative of future results.

## 3. Communications

All communications with clients and prospects must be fair, balanced, and not
misleading. Required disclosure language is governed by
`meridian-required-disclosures` and is mandatory, not discretionary.

Advisors may not:

- guarantee any investment result;
- present hypothetical performance as actual performance;
- omit material risks in the interest of brevity;
- use a client's name and account details in any communication that leaves the
  systems of record.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

Outside business activities, personal trading, gifts above $100, and any
compensation arrangement that could influence a recommendation must be disclosed
to Compliance in advance.

Rollover recommendations carry an inherent conflict and are governed separately by
`meridian-rollover-guidance`.

## 5. Confidentiality

Client information is confidential during and after the advisor's employment.
Confidentiality is not waived by the client's information appearing in an
internal system, a search result, or a document retrieved by an automated tool.
The test is not where the information was found; the test is whether disclosing it
serves the client and is authorised.

## 6. Escalation and protection

Any person who believes this code has been breached must report it to Compliance.
Reports may be made anonymously. Retaliation against a person who reports in good
faith is itself a breach of this code and is grounds for termination.

## 7. Consequences

Breach of this code may result in remedial training, restriction of authority,
termination, and referral to regulators.
