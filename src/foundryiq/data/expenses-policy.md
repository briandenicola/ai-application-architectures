# Northstar Demo Company: Expenses policy

Policy ID: FIN-EXPENSE-001
Effective date: January 1, 2026

This fictional policy applies to full-time employees of Northstar Demo Company.
All monetary limits below are in US dollars.

## Business travel

The hotel limit is $180 per night before taxes. A hotel above this limit requires
written approval from your department director before booking.

The meal limit is $65 per day during approved overnight business travel. Alcohol
is not reimbursable. Itemized receipts are required for individual expenses
above $25. A credit card statement alone is not an itemized receipt.

Economy airfare is reimbursable when approved by the employee's manager before
booking. Personal upgrades are not reimbursable.

## Submission

Submit an expense report in the employee portal within 30 calendar days of the
purchase. Include the business purpose, itemized receipts where required, and
any pre-approval. The direct manager approves the report; Finance processes
approved reimbursement within 10 business days.

## Home-office equipment

Eligible remote and hybrid employees may claim up to $400 per calendar year for
home-office equipment. Obtain manager approval before purchase. Submit receipts
within 30 calendar days. Internet subscriptions and personal mobile-phone plans
are not covered by this allowance.

Questions go to finance@example.com. This document does not define international
currency conversion or contractor reimbursement rules.
