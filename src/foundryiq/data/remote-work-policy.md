# Northstar Demo Company: Remote-work policy

Policy ID: HR-REMOTE-001
Effective date: January 1, 2026

This fictional policy applies to full-time employees of Northstar Demo Company
whose roles are designated hybrid or remote.

## Hybrid work

Hybrid employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Agree on the weekly
schedule with your direct manager. Be available during core collaboration hours,
10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in your assigned office's local time zone.

Working remotely for more than 3 days in a week requires prior approval from
both the direct manager and HR. Fully remote roles follow their approved
employment arrangement rather than the hybrid-day limit.

## Location and equipment

Work from your registered home address within your country of employment.
Working from another country requires HR and Information Security approval
at least 20 business days before the proposed start date.

Use a company-managed device, multifactor authentication, and the company VPN
when accessing internal applications. Do not use shared public computers.

Remote and hybrid employees may be eligible for the annual home-office
equipment allowance. Consult the Expenses policy for the dollar limit,
approval requirements, and submission deadline.

Questions go to hr@example.com. This document does not authorize exceptions
to immigration, tax, or employment requirements.
