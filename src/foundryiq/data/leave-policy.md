# Northstar Demo Company: Leave policy

Policy ID: HR-LEAVE-001
Effective date: January 1, 2026

This fictional policy applies to all full-time employees of Northstar Demo Company.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave each calendar
year. New employees receive a prorated allocation based on their start date.
Up to 5 unused days may carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over
days must be used by March 31.

Request annual leave in the employee portal at least 10 business days before
the first day away. Your direct manager must approve the request before travel
is booked. Requests longer than 10 consecutive working days also require
approval from the department director.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days each calendar year. Notify your manager
before the start of the working day when practical. Sick leave does not carry
over. Absences longer than 3 consecutive working days require a return-to-work
discussion with HR.

## Questions

Contact hr@example.com for policy questions. This document does not define
parental leave, sabbaticals, or policies for contractors.
